The Cassie launch film

The 50-second “From Noise to a Call” film turns Cassie’s product contract into a deterministic Remotion timeline. Product UI and typography remain real assets; motion and audio are generated from code.

System map

video/CassieLaunch.tsx owns scenes, timing, design tokens, motion, and product-image framing.
video/synth.py writes a seeded 48 kHz soundtrack aligned to the scene cuts.
public/video contains render inputs.
artifacts/launch-video contains the review frame, contact sheet, and MP4 master.

Decision flow

The sequence moves from scattered market fragments to grid alignment, then reveals the Desk, selected trade state, thesis and alpha drivers, explicit trade plan, and Cassie wordmark. The same restrained graphite, ivory, gold, green, and coral system is used throughout so product footage and coded graphics feel continuous.

Why deterministic motion

Market copy, values, disclosures, and UI must stay legible and internally consistent. React, SVG, and HTML keep those layers exact while Remotion provides frame-addressable animation. Seeded audio and grain preserve repeatability across review renders.

Verification

  • npm run video:frames checks a representative typography frame.
  • npm run video:render synthesizes audio and renders the 1920×1080 master.
  • ffprobe verifies duration, codecs, resolution, frame rate, and audio sample rate.
  • A seven-second-interval contact sheet checks the whole narrative arc.
