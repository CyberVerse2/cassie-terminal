# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Active traders and professional analysts evaluating routed market ideas. They need enough evidence, market context, and execution detail to decide whether and how to express a trade without reconstructing the idea across multiple screens.

## Product Purpose

Cassie turns curated trader posts into decision-ready, tradable ideas across perps, tokenized stocks, spot tokens, and prediction markets. Success means a trader can understand the source thesis, judge its credibility, inspect its performance, and finalize a paper trade from one coherent workflow.

## Brand Personality

Focused, credible, decisive. Cassie should feel like a consumer trading terminal: information-dense and market-native, but clear enough that the interface helps users reach a decision instead of making them operate the terminal.

## Anti-references

Avoid institutional terminals that expose complexity without hierarchy, generic consumer finance dashboards that hide the underlying case, and decorative fintech interfaces that prioritize visual effects over market information.

## Design Principles

1. Present the investment case and execution plan as one decision flow.
2. Treat every routed idea as a Cassie call; internal extraction and routing classifications must not create separate product categories.
3. Put live market state next to the original idea so performance is immediately legible.
4. Use progressive disclosure for supporting evidence while keeping decision-critical information in view.
5. Prefer compact, familiar trading patterns over novel interaction models.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Do not rely on color alone for direction or status, preserve keyboard-visible focus, maintain readable contrast at terminal densities, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
